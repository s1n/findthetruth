The light was fading when he got bored with the three-dee. He got bored very
easily these days. The vastness of New Angeles spread out before him, the
high-rises topped with a golden crown from the setting sun. Thomas thought about
changing the view, throwing up a mosh pit or a war zone. Instead he heard the
whisper of the penthouse door, and Helen entered the room.

"Is there anything I can do for you?" she purred, her eyes a crystal blue. She
draped a finger along her lips, and paused, waiting for his response. *Almost
perfect,* he thought. The programming was really quite remarkable--he would know.

"I didn't think mother would let you out of the lab."

"This is our...little secret." She took a step toward him, her hands running along
the form-fitting white mini dress that clung to her manufactured curves, a lone
strand of blonde hair straying across her forehead. The quality of this model was
high, the highest Haas-Bioroid had ever produced. Billions in neural
nano-processers and synthetic flesh. The brain imaging alone cost about the same
as a year's supply of helium-3. Thomas realized he was thinking of Helen as "her,"
as "she," not "it." A bioroid that can pass as human.

He touched the PAD worn on his wrist and they were surrounded by a fire-lit beach,
a thousand stars twinkling overhead. "Helen," he said, and he leaned into her,
their lips almost touching, his hand cradling her face. "How about a nice little
jaunt upstalk?"

These strobe lights were real. The Orange Room of The Castle Club was one of the
most exclusive in the world; no androids allowed, under any circumstances. But
they breezed right past the anti-android protocols. Thomas watched Helen out of
the corner of his eye chat up some ugly old man. He had already forgotten the name
of the woman he was gossiping with. He nodded politely as she whispered something
in his ear, and she withdrew, looking hurt. It didn't matter. He felt like he was
living in a dream, and he was on the edge of waking up.

*A bioroid can pass as human.* With Helen, the sky was the limit. She cast her
beautiful blue eyes toward him, and he could no longer see the machine behind the
smile. *Is she real?* She blinked, and for a split second he saw himself reflected
in silver pupils.

He grabbed at the back of his neck, digging his manicured nails into his flesh.
They came away caked in flakes of skin. He breathed a sigh of relief. What a
stupid notion.

*A bioroid can pass as human.*

Time to get drunk.
