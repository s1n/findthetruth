Sunny Lebeau drops her children at daycare, goes to work at Globalsec, and takes a grey-hat job testing the security of an NBN storage service.

---

“Good-bye, sweeties,” she said. Five-year-old Frankie was already running at a full sprint for the playground but Alexa held back. At three, she was more cautious than her brother and still not enthusiastic about daycare.

“Mommy,” she said, “what do you do all day? Why can’t you stay?”

“Mommy works on the 72nd floor right up there,” Sunny said, pointing up at the looming miniarc above them. The Globalsec tower was smack-dab in the Laguna Velasco district, not far from the NAPD. “Mommy works on the Network to keep people safe.”

Alexa gave Mommy a kiss and then shuffled off, taking a chunk of Sunny’s heart with her. With Pat on Mars for two years now, she was on her own. Not for the first time she considered giving it all up and staying home with the children.

She thought about it during the ride up to the 72nd floor. She was still thinking about it when the doors opened and she stepped out into the offices of the Intercorp Task Force. Her boss, Jak Sinclair, was waiting for her.

“Good morning, Lebeau,” he said. He fell into step beside her as she walked down the row of cockpits, where other Globalsec runners were jacked in or browsing data between runs. “Got a black hat sniffing around our servers earlier. Security defect on the latest Qianju hoppers, you might want to take a look at that, but our top story today is…” He gestured at her Nexus as she reached her pod, at the end of the row. It bloomed to life and showed the NBN logo.

“So is my hat white or grey on this one?” Sunny asked. Sometimes Globalsec was asked to test another corp’s security—a white hat, purely legal job. Other times Sunny found herself part of an investigation that required breaking in where she wasn’t wanted. Those were the fun jobs.

“Grey. We have a tip that NBN is sniffing data off one of their supposedly secure storage services. That would put them in breach of contract.” He smiled and sipped his coffee. “Chase tested this same branch last month. Couldn’t get in. Their security is as good as anything I’ve seen.”

She settled her visor over her head and stepped up to the Nexus. “I should have something for you by lunch.”

“You’re the best, Lebeau,” said Jak, drifting back toward his office.

“‘Mommy, what do you do all day?’” she murmured to herself as she pulled on her ViScape H3 goggles. “Mommy kicks ass and takes names, sweetie.” She grinned and jacked in.
