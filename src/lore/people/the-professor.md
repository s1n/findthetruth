He couldn't remember the last time he'd had this many students attend a lecture in
person. The hall was almost full, and every empty seat was occupied by a
shimmering 3D avatar. A quick check of the roster showed more students virtually
present, their avatars overridden by the presence of meatspace bodies.

Atoc put on his best Professorial smile. "Hello, everyone," he said. "Welcome to
Distributive Weighted Logic 402. I say 'welcome' because there are more people in
the room than are technically enrolled in this class. I deduce that for many of
you it's your first time here."

A nervous chuckle ran through the room. He waved his hand and the virt display
behind him bloomed to life with an image from one of NBN's newsfeeds: him outside
the courthouse. "I have, however, exceeded my patience with the nosies, so if you
are a journalist please leave now. I promise you, the lecture you will be forced to
endure is not worth the potential for a juicy quote." Another chuckle, and Atoc
smiled again. "I'm not joking. Your recording equipment has now been disabled."

There was a low murmur as the meatspace crowd poked and prodded at their PADs and
other equipment. Three stood to go, their vacant seats promptly filled by virt
students.

"That's better." He gestured and the virt played a silent vid of his arrest. "Yes,
I was arrested two days ago. No, I have not been fired. Tenure! Yes, most of my
research funding has been pulled. No, I will not be canceling any of my classes. If
that covers everything, then I will begin the lecture."

"Why'd you do it? I thought HB funded your research! Do you hate them?" It was a
fresh in the first row, the only student he remembered attending every lecture in
meatspace. Atoc's secretary projected the kid's name onto his spectacles--Garcia.

"You're presuming that I am guilty of the act of which I was accused, Mr. Garcia."
Atoc paused, considering. The class was paying better attention now than ever
before. "Hatred is one reason that...someone...might do such a thing. But I prefer
to be constructive. To build something, preserve something. The Quechua bloodline
is alive and well, of course, mixed and mingled throughout the Americas. But
Quechua language, culture, and history all stand on the brink of extinction. If
someone was to model not just a single AI, but an entire culture of
intelligences--a historic world preserved in virtual space--that would be an
unparalleled achievement."

"And you needed proprietary mind-mapping resources to finish the project..." mused
Mr. Garcia. "Not that you did it!" he hastily added.

"Not that I did it," said Atoc, and smiled. "Sync your readers, everyone, we're
really going to get started now..." It was a good lecture. Atoc made a note to find
out everything he could about Mr. Garcia for later.
