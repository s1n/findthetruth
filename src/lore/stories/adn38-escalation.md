## Escalation

Eiko perched on top of a crumbling concrete blockhouse not far from Antiguo Guayaquil. With her
enhanced vision, she had an excellent view of the undercity street below and the squat, sprawling
buildings in the shadow of the arks above. With her enhanced hearing, she could clearly make out
the gunfire and screaming coming from the far side of the block. Someone had beat her to the
target. Someone with a much more flexible definition of "subtlety."

She flicked her left wrist and projected the virt image of her target one last time: Greg Logan,
lawyer, NBN Surveillance Contracts Team, hair blond, skin brown, height 172 centimeters. She
didn't know why Jinteki wanted him, and didn't care. Her part wasn't to question.

Eiko raised her right arm and fired a grapnel from her wrist-mount, securing it onto a strut
supporting a slidewalk above her, then swung across the gap to the next building. The grapnel
released and then retracted back up her sleeve as she ran toward the firefight.

Two sec teams were shooting it out at the front entrance of a dilapidated hotel, one easily a
century old. It had been used as an NBN safehouse, but apparently whatever tip drew Eiko here had
reached other interested parties as well. The yellow-armored NBN security team was pinned down by
a larger force wearing badgeless black armor. Eiko could hear movement beneath her as more
members of the sec team moved through her building to attack the hotel from a new direction. If
there were any civilians in the area, they were not making an effort to slow the progress of the
sec team.

Eiko paused, crouching in the shadows. She took a deep breath, launched her grapnel again, and
swung across onto the hotel. She ran forward, hearing shouting, feeling a bullet pass close
by—fired by which team? She didn't know. Then she was lost in the shadows, again, moving past an
outdated rattling old air conditioning unit and emerging in the shadow of a hopper pad. A single
security trooper in NBN yellow stood on the platform, facing towards the transplas doors of the
hotel's roof access. Eiko rolled under the pad, came up beneath the trooper, and drew a carbon
knife from under her other sleeve. She stabbed him from beneath—deep into his thigh—and caught
him as he fell. She left the knife in the wound to stem the bleeding; the poison killed him
before his next heartbeat anyway.

By the time Logan and his escort came out of the doors, Eiko was wearing an NBN uniform and
standing guard over the recently arrived hopper. "Mr. Logan," she said, opening one gull-wing
door. "This way." Logan climbed in. When the first of his two escorts made to follow, she shot him
through the head, then shot the other. She slid into the hopper and leveled her gun at the Tenma
clone at the yoke.

"Nihongai District," she said, in Japanese. "Manual control." The clone blanched, and nodded, and
the hopper lifted off.

"What is happening?" said Logan. "Who are you?"

"Just another samurai," said Eiko.
