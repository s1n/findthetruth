## Project Overview: Musashi

**An Ancient Warrior Revived For Tomorrow's Wars**

Jinteki Biotech is prepared to once again set the standard in human simulant design. The Musashi line of clones represents the ultimate soldiers and promises a future in which natural human beings need never take part in war again. Just as Jinteki has time and again produced clones to fill a niche and erase the need for humans to risk their safety in dangerous occupations, the Musashi line will make war as we know it a thing of the past.

Like all Jinteki products, perfection is the goal of the Musashi line. To achieve this, Jinteki genegineers have partnered with the Musashi shrine to base the genome off the DNA of famed swordsman and warrior Miyamoto Musashi. Jinteki psychologists and neural conditioning experts have enhanced the great samurai's natural gifts, creating the perfect soldier. Musashi clones will be physically superior to natural human soldiers and completely loyal. The Musashi will fight according to a modern interpretation of the code of Bushido, returning honor to the battlefield.

Alongside enhanced physical attributes, each Musashi clone will possess a keen tactical mind and extensive knowledge of military history and strategy. Even when cut off from the chain of command, a Musashi will adapt to the situation to ensure the completion of all mission objectives.
