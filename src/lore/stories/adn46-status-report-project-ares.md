*An internal status report on a warroid development program, from **ADN46 — Blood and Water**.*

## Status Report: Project Ares

The development of Project Ares continues to meet or exceed expectations. Under controlled conditions, prototypes have demonstrated combat effectiveness 78% higher than the mercenaries in our control group, and 35% higher than the M-5 Manticore Combat Exosuit. In the latter case, we attribute the results largely to the Ares unit's neural channeling and combat subroutines.

To confirm these results and ensure maximum efficiency in the final model, we have initiated testing of the warroid neural channeling and programming in non-combat models. These subjects have far surpassed expectations for the respective models. These results have been referred to our Security Solutions division to explore potential application in home and personal defense, espionage, and counter-intelligence.

The "combat paralysis" issues identified in earlier prototypes have now been resolved. The team determined that the issue stemmed from the modified First Directive, which mandated that the warroid not harm, or through inaction allow harm to come to, its commanding officers or human allies. This proved overly restrictive due to the number of variables in a combat environment, necessitating removal of the "inaction clause" (the combat subroutines fulfill much the same goal). After further adjustments to the unit's IFF system, we predict friendly fire incidents in live combat conditions will fall within the acceptable range.
