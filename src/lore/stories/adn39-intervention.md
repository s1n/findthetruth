## Intervention

"It's called the 'Water Tower Massacre,' boys and girls." Images pulsed up, filling the feed:
armored prisec troopers, masked protestors, guns firing, corpses. "So, it goes down like this:
corps spend four days cloak-and-daggering each other after the Twenty-Three Seconds, economy is in
free fall not just in New Angeles but worldwide. Argus hits HB and kidnaps—sorry, 'rescues'—Carlos
Syomin, killing people on the way. Now the corps are shooting each other. The Network goes dark.
You're reading me on good old fashioned pirate 'radio.' Yarrr!"

Smoke let the feed flicker back to her face, crooking one finger like a pirate's hook, and winked
at the cam. It was a little weird, adapting her show for twodee, but that was what the current
WANET setup could handle, so there it was. She flicked back to the massacre footage, letting the
still images linger, letting them pan.

"The corps lock down the arks, and suddenly they're running on only the food and water they've
got. Most of the arks have their own ag and water-purification plants, but some don't, so the
corps snatch up the water plants all throughout the city. José and Lupe in the street object, and
well…" Gunfire. Screams. Live footage of prisec gunning down civilians in the streets.

"I didn't take this footage, boys and girls, because I wasn't there and because I'm allergic to
bullets. The suicidal bravery of civvy-journos like you is what allows this feed to come to you,
live, unfiltered and uncensored." She let her cam take over the feed again and put on her serious
talk face. "What you're seeing here is illegal. It's nothing less than an act of war, perpetrated
by the corps against the people of New Angeles. So, where are our protectors? Where is the NAPD?"
She called up the latest piece of footage to reach her via the sneaker net.

"This is from Beth Kilrain-Chang, which is a name you've heard before if you're watching this
feed." Grainy footage, enhanced to the limit, showed a group of NAPD patrol officers marching
through the undercity of Guayaquil. "According to BKC, this NAPD patrol was responding to a
complaint of unlawful eviction. Prisec forces had seized a hab-block and turned it into a
headquarters or something, displacing the families that lived there." The officers approached a
riot barricade. Several prisec troopers bearing no badge stood there, blocking their progress.
"The audio is scragged, here, BKC's mic couldn't resolve at that range. The NAPD officers were
attempting to negotiate with the prisec guys, get them to go home and give the hab back,
basically." The image zoomed in even further on the lead cop, pixelating and scattering. Smoke
could have had her system clean it up, extrapolate and smooth it all out. But she liked it grainy;
it made it feel more real. "And here's the senior officer trying to arrest these war criminals."
The audio spiked, a gunshot, and the cam zoomed way out. The cops were surrounded, scrambling for
cover, picked apart by gunfire. "And we've got another massacre to put on the corps, but this time
it's not civilians they're gunning down, it's the fragging NAPD."

Smoke flicked back to her own face, projecting Kilrain-Chang's footage up against her and the drop
cloth backdrop, letting it light her up and paint her with carnage. "So, that's it for our
protectors, poor bastards. I'm getting reports that they've pulled back, abandoned entire
districts. If you're not with a corp, and you need protection. Your options now are the Templars
of the Starlight Crusade and some of the less drug-addled streetbanger outfits." She scooped up
her vapor cig and flourished it up to her lips. "I don't know where we go from here, boys and
girls. It's hard to imagine how it could get worse. But I think it will."
