Criminals are in it for themselves. All runners are technically criminals, at
least if you ask the corps, but these runners embrace it. They make self-interest
an art form and don't care who gets hurt so long as they get ahead. Many
Criminals engage in more traditional forms of crime as well, stealing data and
money with equal gusto. Criminals are good at covering their tracks and employing
a variety of dirty tricks to attack from an unexpected angle.
